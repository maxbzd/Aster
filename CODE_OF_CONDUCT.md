# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Aster to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps build a good community:

- being kind and patient, and assuming good intent;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

Unacceptable behavior:

- harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and discriminatory jokes or language;
- sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome advances;
- publishing others' private information without permission;
- trolling, sustained disruption, or anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct,
and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment or open a private report as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
and mark it as a conduct report. Reports are handled in confidence.

This applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project in public.
